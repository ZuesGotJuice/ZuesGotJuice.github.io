Online trust is broken.

Fake profiles, scammers, bots, and impersonation continue to impact platforms and their users every day.

VeriDate is building identity verification and trust technology designed to help platforms reduce fraud and build safer online experiences.

Identity Verification & Authenticity Signals

Platforms need more than simple verification. VeriDate combines identity checks, phone verification, liveness detection, and behavioral signals into an authenticity confidence layer — helping platforms reduce fake accounts, scams, and impersonation.

Security & Privacy First

VeriDate is being built with privacy and security as core principles.

  • Industry-standard security practices are being implemented throughout development.
  • Privacy and data minimization are core product principles.
  • Enterprise-grade security and compliance requirements are part of our long-term product roadmap.

(We do not claim SOC 2, GDPR certification, HIPAA, or ISO compliance.)
