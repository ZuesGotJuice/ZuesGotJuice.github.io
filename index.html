Online trust is broken. Fake profiles, scammers, bots, and impersonation continue to impact digital platforms and their users.

VeriDate is building identity verification and trust intelligence technology designed to help platforms create safer online experiences.

Identity Verification & Authenticity Signals

VeriDate combines identity checks, phone verification, liveness detection, and behavioral trust signals into an authenticity confidence layer designed for modern online platforms.

How VeriDate Works

1. User Verification

Users complete identity and authenticity verification checks.

2. Trust Intelligence

Verification results and trust signals are analyzed.

3. Platform Protection

Platforms receive confidence signals to help reduce fraud.
